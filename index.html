Watch it in motion

Neon Drift is a fast, reflex-driven arcade flyer soaked in a Miami-neon glow. You guide a glowing pilot through an endless gauntlet of pulsing gates — it's easy to pick up, but every run gets faster, with tighter gaps, streaking projectiles, and tunnel corridors that never let you settle. It's the kind of game you drop into for one quick run and look up an hour later.

Unlike a one-button flyer, Neon Drift uses a dual-zone tap: tap the top of the screen to flap down, the bottom to flap up. That direct up-and-down control is what gives the game its skill ceiling — you can hover, feint, and thread gaps a single-button game never could. The full breakdown is on the How to Play page.

Five ways to fly

  • Classic
  • Daily Challenge
  • Projectile Storm
  • Versus
  • Last Pilot Standing

Chase your best in endless Classic, take the seeded Daily Challenge that everyone plays the same way, dodge a barrage in Projectile Storm, duel a friend in Versus, or drop into a live Last Pilot Standing battle royale where outlasting the field earns you the chance to win — you have to out-fly their score too. Classic, Storm, the Daily and Last Pilot Standing each have their own leaderboard, and every mode has its own kind of pressure — see them all on the Game Modes page.

Earn as you fly

Almost everything feeds progression. Runs earn XP that levels you up, high scores unlock a ladder of achievement bird skins, and there are elaborate animated premium skins, hidden mystery-trail effects, customizable pipe colors and animated designs, decorated tunnel corridors earned through Daily Challenge streaks, and unlockable backgrounds. Two rotating daily missions hand out bonus XP every day. The full list lives on the Unlocks & Skins page.

New gameplay and feature videos go up on the Neon Drift YouTube channel.

Free, and built to keep you coming back

Neon Drift is completely free to play, right in the browser — no download and no account needed to jump in. Link an account and you can back up your XP and skins, add friends by callsign, and carry your progress across devices, but that's entirely optional. With a fresh Daily Challenge, two new daily missions, a weekly leaderboard that resets every Monday, a streak to keep alive, and a live battle royale available every day, there's always a reason to fly one more run.

Neon Drift is free to play in any modern browser, with mobile versions on the way. No install and no sign-up required — just tap and fly.
