NEON DRIFT

A fast, reflex-fueled arcade flyer soaked in Miami-neon.

▶ Play Now — Free in Your Browser

Drift through a glowing synthwave gauntlet at pure reflex speed. Tap the top half of the screen to flap down, the bottom half to flap up, and thread your neon bird through an endless run of pipes, tunnels, and escalating hazards. Every run is a chance to climb the global leaderboards and unlock new looks.

Five Ways to Play

Classic

Pipes, tunnels, and rising difficulty across seven waves — the original run.

Daily Challenge

One seeded course a day. Everyone plays the exact same run — settle who's best.

Projectile Storm

No pipes — just a nonstop, ever-escalating wave of incoming fire.

Versus

Challenge a friend to a seeded run and see who lasts longer.

Last Pilot Standing

Up to 10 pilots drop into the same seeded run — last one alive wins.

Unlock Everything

Earn dozens of bird skins and animated pipe designs by hitting score milestones, surviving specific challenges, and winning matches. Premium skins, exclusive trails, and a supporter bundle are available in the in-game store — and your unlocks sync across devices when you link an account.

Play Anywhere

Neon Drift runs instantly in any modern browser, and is also available as a native app on Android and iOS. Progress and leaderboards carry across all of them.

About

Neon Drift is an independent game built by Brandon Clevenger. It's free to play, with optional cosmetic purchases and an optional rewarded-ad retry on the Daily Challenge. No account is required to play — sign-in only backs up your callsign and unlocks. Questions? Head to the support page.
