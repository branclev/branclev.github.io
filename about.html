About Neon Drift

The story behind the game.

Hi, my name is Brandon. I've always wanted to make games, and I finally decided to go for it when my nephews started showing a lot of interest in games. A lot of what they played seemed overly complicated, so I wanted to build something simple and competitive — inspired by a simple, competitive game I used to play when I was their age.

Neon Drift is the result: a fast, reflex-driven neon arcade flyer that anyone can pick up in seconds, with leaderboards and challenges to keep it interesting. I hope you enjoy playing it as much as my nephews and I have.

What Neon Drift is

Neon Drift is a single-player and multiplayer arcade game where you guide a glowing pilot through an endless neon gauntlet. It features daily challenges, global leaderboards, unlockable cosmetics, and head-to-head modes — all wrapped in a Miami-neon aesthetic.
