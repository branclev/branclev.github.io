What the percentile filter does

The top 1% and top 10% filters apply to the all-time Classic board only — that is the board the in-game [1%] and [10%] titles are derived from, so it is the only place the bands mean anything. The cut is made on score, not on rank, so everyone sharing the boundary score qualifies together and nobody loses a place to a tiebreak. The population counts players who have posted a score above zero.

Those bands are live standings, not permanent awards. Players who equip the matching title in game keep it only while they remain in range — drop out and the badge comes off, climb back and it returns.

About the titles you'll see

A badge before a pilot's name is a title. [OG] marks an account linked before launch and can never be earned again once the game is out. [1%] and [10%] are the leaderboard bands above. Titles are optional — plenty of players have one and choose not to wear it, so an unbadged name near the top is not a mistake. Read more on Unlocks & Skins.
