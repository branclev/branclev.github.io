Contact

Every message comes straight to the developer.

Neon Drift is a one-person project, so there's no support queue and no ticket system. You email me, and I reply. The address is Brandon@neondriftgame.com.

I try to answer everything, usually within a couple of days. If something is broken and affecting your progress, say so in the subject line and I'll look at it first.

What's worth writing about

  • Bugs. Especially anything that cost you a run, a score or an unlock. These get priority.
  • Purchases. If something didn't arrive, didn't restore, or was charged twice.
  • Accounts and data. Sign-in trouble, a callsign problem, or a data request.
  • Ideas. A good chunk of what's in the game came from someone saying "it would be better if…". That's not a courtesy — several features exist because a player asked.
  • Something that feels unfair. Difficulty, a leaderboard result, an unlock that seems impossible. Worth telling me even if you're not sure it's a bug.
  • Creators and press. Happy to help with footage, keys or questions.

Details that help me help you

  • Your in-game callsign — it's how I find your account.
  • What you were doing when it happened, and whether it repeats.
  • Your device and browser, or whether you're in the app. A surprising number of issues are specific to one or the other.
  • For purchases, the approximate date and what you bought.
  • A screenshot, if there's anything to see. Usually worth more than a description.

Reporting a leaderboard or fairness problem

The leaderboards are the reason this game exists, so anything that undermines them matters more than most bugs. If you see a score that looks impossible, a match that resolved wrongly, or a name that's impersonating someone, send it to me with the callsign and roughly when you saw it.

Every score is checked server-side and there are limits on what the game will accept, but no system catches everything, and a real person spotting something is often faster than the automated checks. You don't need proof — a screenshot and a rough time is plenty for me to go looking.

What I can't do

A few things fall outside what I control, and it saves us both time to say so:

  • Refunds for mobile purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple, not by me — I can't issue or reverse one. I can help work out what happened and make sure you end up with what you paid for.
  • Restoring a deleted account. Deletion is permanent and immediate by design. That's the promise the deletion page makes, and quietly keeping a copy would make it a lie.
  • Transferring progress between two accounts you own. Progress follows the account it was earned on.

What happens to your email

It goes to a personal inbox and gets read by me. I don't run a mailing list, I won't add you to one, and I won't pass your address to anyone. If you write in about a bug I may ask a follow-up question or two; if you'd rather not go back and forth, say so and I'll work with what you've sent. What the game itself collects is a separate matter, covered in the privacy policy.
