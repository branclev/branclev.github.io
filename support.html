Support

Answers to the things people ask most.

If none of this covers it, email Brandon@neondriftgame.com — it reaches me directly. Include your in-game callsign, and for anything involving a purchase, roughly when it happened.

Accounts and progress

I got a new device — where are my purchases and progress?

Sign in with the same account you linked before, then open the Store in the mobile app and tap Restore Purchases. Your callsign, leaderboard history and purchased content come back with it.

Do I have to make an account?

No. You can play the entire game without signing in. Linking an account is what lets your progress survive a reinstall or move between devices, and it's required to appear on the global leaderboards — but it's your choice.

What's a callsign?

Your name on every leaderboard and in every match. It's how other players see you and how I find your account if you write in.

How do I delete my data?

If your callsign is linked to an account: Settings → Profile → Danger Zone → Delete My Data. Full details, including what is and isn't removed, are on the data deletion page.

Daily Challenge and streaks

How does the Daily Challenge work?

Everyone in the world flies the same seeded course each day — identical obstacles in an identical order. You get one run at the global daily board, and it resets at midnight UTC. Same course for everyone is the whole point: it makes the scores directly comparable instead of a story about who got a kinder layout.

What's a daily streak?

Consecutive days you've completed the Daily Challenge. Streaks unlock some of the better cosmetics in the game, so they're worth protecting. If you enable notifications, you'll get a reminder on a day you haven't played yet and your streak is at risk.

I lost my streak — can I get it back?

Sometimes. There's a limited streak-restore option in the game. If something clearly went wrong on my end, write in and I'll take a look.

Challenges

How do I challenge someone?

Finish a Classic run and tap CHALLENGE A FRIEND. You get a link to the exact course you just flew, with your score on it. Send it however you like.

What does the other person get?

The identical course — same pipes, same tunnels, same order — and your score to beat. Both scores are shown side by side at the end, and they can send it back. If they have the Android app installed, the link opens it directly.

Do challenge runs count for anything?

Yes. The first time you fly a given course it counts like a Classic run for XP, levels and unlocks. Replaying the same course still earns XP but stops counting toward stats, so nobody can memorise one course and farm it. Challenge runs never go on a leaderboard.

Can I challenge someone to the Daily?

No, and that is deliberate. Everyone gets one attempt at the same ranked course, so a link to it would let people practise before their real run.

My link doesn't open the app.

It should on Android if the app is installed. If it opens a browser instead, the game still works there and your progress follows your account either way.

Seasonal skins

How do I get a seasonal skin?

Fly 40 runs while that season's window is open. Every mode counts — Classic, Daily, Storm, Versus and Last Pilot Standing. Once earned it is yours permanently.

When are the windows?

Each runs 21 days and closes the day after its holiday: JACK around Halloween, GOBBLE around Thanksgiving, TINSEL around Christmas, CUPID around Valentine's and BUNNY around Easter. The Skins screen shows your progress and the days remaining while one is open.

I missed one. Have I lost it forever?

No. Seasons return every year. You will get another chance at the same skin next time the window comes round.

My runs are not counting.

Runs only count while a window is actually open, and the game checks that against the server rather than your device clock — so changing your phone's date will not open one. If a window is open and your count is not moving, check your connection and tell me.

Unlocks, levels and cosmetics

How do I unlock skins, pipes and tunnel art?

Almost everything is earned by playing — scores, levels, daily streaks, podium finishes, multiplayer wins and total runs flown. A small number of cosmetics are available as optional purchases in the mobile app. None of them affect gameplay.

What happens at max level?

The level cap is 100. Past that you enter prestige, shown as chevrons next to your name, up to a tenth tier with its own marker. Prestige is cosmetic — it's a record of time invested, not an advantage.

What are titles?

Tags you can display next to your callsign, earned for specific achievements — some for scores, some for finishing in the top percentage of players. You can see every title and its requirement in the game, including ones you haven't earned yet, so you know what to chase.

Ads and purchases

Are there ads?

Not currently. When ads do arrive they will never be mandatory and never interrupt you — you'll never be made to watch one to continue playing or to get back to the menu. Optional, rewarded, and always your choice. That's a design rule, not a current limitation; more on the reasoning on the about page.

What do purchases actually get me?

Cosmetics and supporter status. Nothing you can buy makes you faster, gives you extra lives, or improves your score. A paying player and a free player compete on exactly the same terms — which matters, because the leaderboards are the point of the game.

What is a combo pack?

Three cosmetics from one idea bought together — a pipe interior, tunnel art and a background that all belong to the same place — rather than three separate unlocks. The first is Koi Pond. Buying it unlocks all three pieces at once, and they then behave exactly like anything you earned: equip them separately, mix them with anything else, change them whenever you like.

I bought a combo pack and only some of it showed up.

All three pieces unlock together, so this should not happen. If it does, try Restore Purchases in the Store, and check under PIPES, TUNNELS and BACKGROUND on the Skins screen — the three pieces live in three different places rather than in one bundle. If something is still missing, email me with your callsign.

Something I bought didn't arrive.

Try Restore Purchases in the Store first. If it still isn't there, email me with your callsign and the approximate date and I'll sort it out.

Playing and performance

Browser or app — is there a difference?

It's the same game and the same code. The app adds purchases, notifications and offline launching. Progress follows your account either way.

The game feels choppy on my phone.

Close other tabs and apps first — a browser game shares the device with everything else running. Battery-saver modes can also throttle it. If it's still rough, tell me your exact device and what you were doing; performance reports are taken seriously here, and a few have led to real fixes written up in the articles.

One known behaviour worth naming: on iPhone, iOS itself drops a frame on every screen touch. That is measurable on a page containing nothing but a canvas and a single listener, so it is not something the game is doing, and not something it can fix. If you notice a very small hitch on each tap, that is what it is.

My score didn't appear on the leaderboard.

Leaderboard scores need a linked account and a live connection. If both were in place and it still didn't show, send me your callsign and roughly when you played.
